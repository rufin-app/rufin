<p>Enjoy your music collection wherever it lives: on your computer, in shared folders or on your own music server.</p>
<ul>
<li>Supports Jellyfin, Navidrome, Plex and Emby servers, local folders, Nextcloud and network shares</li>
<li>Internet radio stations with artwork and lyrics where available</li>
<li>Rufin Connect securely syncs your sources, playlists, favorites and listening history across devices, lets you continue or control playback on another device, and play your local music library on all of them</li>
<li>Gapless playback or crossfade, and even volume across songs</li>
<li>Graphic and parametric equalizer with presets, and surround sound output</li>
<li>Next track recommendations with Auto DJ, Mix with filters and sleep timer</li>
<li>Downloads with automatic rules and transcoding</li>
<li>Playlists across sources, and smart playlists built on metadata and listening activity</li>
<li>Synced lyrics with karaoke mode, Japanese readings, search/edit/automatically save lyrics</li>
<li>Edit song details and artwork for many tracks at once, or recognize songs by their sound and fill in their details from MusicBrainz</li>
<li>Last.fm, Libre.fm, ListenBrainz and Discord Rich Presence integrations</li>
<li>Casting to UPnP and Chromecast, a Web UI and an HTTP API for scripts</li>
<li>Custom themes, a mini player, fullscreen visualizer and an extensive keyboard shortcuts catalog</li>
<li>Plays nearly every audio format, including albums split by cue sheets</li>
</ul>
